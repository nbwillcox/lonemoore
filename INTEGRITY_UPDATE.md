# Lonemoore dungeon integrity and navigation update

16 September 2026. Project: `J:\First Person Dungeon Crawler Game`.

## What changed

Movement previously checked the destination tile, while generated alternate passages could reach the stair enclosure without crossing the intended lock. The environment also disabled collision. The repair adds explicit cell boundaries to the existing floor records: movement, line of sight, door animation, geometry, automap and radar now consume those records. Required gates protect every entrance to the stair enclosure. Keys must be acquired and the gate explicitly opened; movement remains blocked until opening finishes. Stair interaction requires standing on the stair tile and still checks the existing guardian requirement.

New games use bounded, validated room-template generation with entrance-side loops, a reachable key chamber, optional lever-operated stores, secrets, and a protected sanctuary. Stored legacy layouts retain every tile; migration adds explicit boundaries and seals access to their stair enclosure. Saved layouts are never silently regenerated. Unlocked/open doors, switches, secrets, map markers, exploration snapshots, seed/content version and existing progression persist in the existing SaveGame system.

The **M map** supports north-up orientation, facing, remembered versus visible geometry, middle-mouse pan, wheel zoom, recentering, visited-floor browsing and click-to-toggle markers. A compact framed radar shares the map transform and discovery data. Enemy detection uses short range plus line of sight at five updates per second. Unseen objects retain their last observed map state; closed secret passages appear as walls. Map interaction consumes the input without moving the party.

## Playable environment and artwork

Blender **5.2** exported 19 new modules; Unreal **5.8.2** imported and saved them. The imported floor is 400 cm across and the door is 302 cm wide. `ArtReview/IntegrityKit/Lonemoore_Integrity_Kit.blend` preserves the editable source. Metric UVs, bevels and module pivots are generated by `Tools/build_integrity_kit.py`; `Tools/import_integrity_kit.py` repeats import and regional-material construction.

Playable geometry includes instanced floors, thick walls, recessed tomb niches, arches, vaulted ceilings, independently animated gates, and regional burial, sewer, camp and ritual props. Continuous floor/wall/ceiling collision seals cover decorative module seams. Doorway collision keeps its opening. Original shrine, enemy, key and character artwork remains in use. Flickering braziers, drifting dust and localized synthesized stone, drip and infernal ambience provide restrained motion and sound. Existing Lumen and virtual-shadow settings were retained; Nanite was not enabled for these small modules.

The supplied `.art/splashscreen.png` and `.art/loadingscreen.png` are installed in the startup flow. The native splash is staged as `Content/Splash/Splash.bmp`; its packaged copy has an identical SHA-256. The loading artwork keeps its proportions and displays before the menu. The current packaged loading screen was captured and inspected at `Saved/IntegrityUpdate/Packaged/loading.png`. See `STARTUP_ART.md` for the startup-specific evidence.

## Remaining limits

The new environments are integrated development art. They provide real architectural depth and regional dressing, but final sculpted statues, bespoke material textures, richer regional room silhouettes, wear decals and final audio remain listed in `_MISSING_ART_LIST.md`. The approved concept art's final fidelity is not claimed. The exported pillar and new stair modules are available in the kit; existing stair presentation is retained in the game.

Performance figures are short samples on this machine, with current graphics settings, not a full campaign benchmark. Automated pointer events exercise the real widget handlers; they do not constitute a human playtest. Native desktop splash visibility was not independently verified; its staged file was verified and the loading screen was rendered in the current packaged review. Existing input-review API deprecation warnings remain compatible with Unreal 5.8.2.
