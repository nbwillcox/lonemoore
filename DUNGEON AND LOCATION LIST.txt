DUNGEON AND LOCATION LIST

## TLDR

Build **one clickable town hub**, **four progressively deeper dungeon tiers**, and **Hell as the final region**. Here’s a proposed **30-location art list**, with each area visually distinct.

# 1. Main Town

*A lived-in medieval refuge: warm lanterns, weathered stone, timber buildings, and danger waiting beneath it.*

| #  | Location               | Artwork direction / purpose                                                                                                      |
| -- | ---------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 01 | **Main Town Overview** | Wide, clickable town square. Clearly recognizable shops, central hunt board, dungeon entrance, and a slightly tucked-away alley. |
| 02 | **Job / Hunt Board**   | Heavy wooden noticeboard covered with contracts, bounty posters, monster sketches, and wax seals.                                |
| 03 | **Blacksmith**         | Open forge, glowing coals, anvil, weapon racks, armor stands, and a soot-covered smith. Weapons and armor services.              |
| 04 | **Merchant’s Shop**    | Crowded shelves of potions, herbs, lanterns, rope, packs, and adventuring supplies. A welcoming but calculating shopkeeper.      |
| 05 | **Healer’s Sanctuary** | Peaceful stone chapel with candles, a restorative fountain, and a glowing resurrection altar.                                    |
| 06 | **Tavern & Inn**       | Warm hearth, heavy tables, adventurers’ belongings, and stairs leading to rented rooms. A cozy contrast to the dungeon.          |
| 07 | **Back Alley**         | Narrow passage beside the shops. Easy to overlook initially, but visibly accessible; a dim lantern hints at activity.            |
| 08 | **Gambler’s Hideaway** | Shady dealer at a small table beneath an awning. Dice, cards, mysterious equipment, and locked strongboxes.                      |
| 09 | **Dungeon Entrance**   | Ancient fortified stairwell descending beneath the town. Rusted gates, warning signs, and abandoned adventuring gear.            |

**Healer rule:** Players can receive ordinary healing or choose resurrection instead of recovering their corpse. **That resurrection forfeits the items in their lost inventory.**

**Gambler visibility:** The alley should be discoverable by looking around the town—not through an invisible hotspot or an obscure puzzle.

## Shared Artwork Needed Within Each Dungeon Theme

Each theme also needs matching **straight corridors, corners, intersections, doorways, stairs up/down, treasure rooms, and boss chambers**. These are reusable variations—not additional regions.

**Art standard:** Detailed, sleek medieval dark fantasy. Town artwork uses clickable overview and service scenes; dungeon artwork uses consistent **first-person, eye-level views**. Keep interface buttons, shop labels, and contract text separate from the painted backgrounds.
