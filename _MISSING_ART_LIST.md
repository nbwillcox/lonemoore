# Missing final art

Existing character cards, town scenes, monster images, gear sheets, keys, and shrine images are available. No new raster artwork has been generated.

- **Intro and both ending panels** — story scenes. Needed: the solitary arrival, Astra's defeat / dawn, the betrayal / burning Lonemoore, and the lingering Hell portal. Existing town and entrance art are development stand-ins.
- **Unrepresented enemy families** — later regions. Needed: diseased sewer humanoid, zombie, necromancer, goblin archer, troll, corrupted construct, imp, incubus, greater demon, fallen angel, principality, and devil. Hunt-board illustrations are available for several Hell creatures, but are not transparent monster sprites. Existing monsters are reused where explicitly labelled as development variants.
- **Distinct later-region boss presentations** — any encounter currently reusing a normal monster image needs an approved named-boss image before final art sign-off.
- **Dungeon finish pass** — the integrated IntegrityKit now provides 19 Blender modules, recessed tombs, vaults, framed gates, pipes, drainage channels, ritual and burial props. Final sculpted funerary statues, bespoke regional masonry textures, wear/staining decals, water effects, and more authored room silhouettes remain art polish work. Current regional procedural materials and stylized props are functional development art, not final concept-art fidelity. Editable source: `ArtReview/IntegrityKit/Lonemoore_Integrity_Kit.blend`.

Final audio is also pending. The current music, effects, and localized stone/dripping/infernal ambience are original synthesized development audio.
