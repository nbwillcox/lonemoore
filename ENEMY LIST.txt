ENEMY LIST


## TLDR

**A cursed rat is a perfectly reasonable fantasy enemy.** Mangy fur, glowing eyes, fungal growths, and an aggressive pose can make it disturbing without graphic wounds.

Here’s a **30-enemy roster** for our medieval dungeon crawler, ranging from starting-area pests to bosses.

**Shared render standard:** One enemy per image, transparent background, full body and weapons completely inside the frame, facing the player. Modern, sleek, highly detailed dark-fantasy artwork—not pixel art. Keep spell effects close to the character and exclude scenery.
