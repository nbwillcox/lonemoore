# Remaining development work

- New floors use 68 sections (34.6% fewer) and contain 45–46 regular encounter sites, with each original roster represented at least seven times. All seven starting classes passed the full campaign route using companions, real supplies and paid town recovery. Sustained balance and campaign duration remain untuned; these checks do not establish player comfort or the pacing of full exploration. Enemy combat stats are unchanged; chest item and quality tables have expanded.
- The September 18 polish fixes the missing curved-room floor with Nanite enabled and adds 24 props across nine themes. Twelve packaged floor views were inspected: ten expose continuous floor and two face nearby walls. All 24 prop types were inspected, but the procession banner and armory views are nearly edge-on, so their frontal detail remains incompletely judged. These checks cover selected still views, not every position or streaming state. Further weathering and detail remain art development work; see the [current review](ArtReview/RoomKitPolish/REVIEW.html).
- Seven warmed stationary RTX 3060 captures at 3440 × 1369 output measured 72.3–75.9 FPS, with 99th-percentile frame times of 14.289–15.130 ms. Layouts changed from 104 to 68 sections, so the prior results provide context rather than identical-scene comparisons. These uncapped offscreen measurements do not establish full-route performance or eliminate every hitch, monitor tear or temporal flicker. See the [current performance evidence](Saved/AdventurePolish/performance_release_evidence.json); earlier measurements remain in [build history](BUILD_STATUS.md).
- Intro and ending scenes reuse existing illustrations. Credits are currently a static screen; companion dialogue is concentrated in recruitment and lore. Final panels, scrolling credits, and more contextual dialogue remain.
- Combat now has 33 original sound effects, including separate cues for all 21 active skills. Waveform, import and native routing checks passed; subjective listening and the final mix still need player judgment. Impact motion, floating damage, and visual spell effects remain polish work.
- The smith now shows current/next item power and hero totals. Inventory-to-equipped comparisons outside the smith could still be expanded.
- Automated routes, saves, combat, and rendered screens have been checked. A sustained human playtest is still needed for comfort, pacing, and balance.
- This release uses named-character folders and is intended for fresh New Games. Old flat save files remain on disk but are not listed in the new browser, and retired 104-section room-kit saves are not supported. The packaged walkthrough covers name entry, two characters, multiple manual saves and cross-character loading; clipboard shortcuts and more than six entries through native pagination were not exercised in that walkthrough. Disk failure during creation is covered by an automated failure fixture, not a physical power-loss test.

See `_MISSING_ART_LIST.md` for final asset needs. The original `.art` files are preserved.
