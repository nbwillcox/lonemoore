PLACE PROMPTS:

MAIN TOWN SQUARE
LOCATION:
Main Town Square

SCENE:
The player stands in a compact medieval adventurer town square.

Several major destinations are visually recognizable from this position:
a working blacksmith forge,
a merchant shop,
a small healer sanctuary,
a tavern,
a large dungeon entrance descending underground,
and a wooden job and monster hunt board near the center of town.

A narrow side alley is visible between two buildings but does not immediately draw attention.

The town is busy enough to feel inhabited but not crowded.

The dungeon entrance should be one of the strongest visual landmarks in the scene.


HUNT BOARD
LOCATION:
Adventurer Job and Hunt Board

SCENE:
The player stands directly in front of a large rugged wooden bounty board in the town square.

The board contains many pinned parchment contracts, monster sketches, wax seals, crude drawings, torn notices, and small trophies from previous hunts.

The actual writing on the parchment should be indistinct and unreadable because game UI will overlay the real mission information.

Weapons and adventuring equipment lean nearby.

The town square remains slightly visible behind and around the board.



BLACKSMITH
LOCATION:
Town Blacksmith

SCENE:
The player stands inside an open medieval forge.

A rugged blacksmith stands behind a heavy wooden counter near an anvil and glowing forge.

Weapons hang along the walls:
swords,
axes,
maces,
daggers,
shields,
and polearms.

Several armor pieces and helmets sit on racks.

Orange forge light illuminates dark stone walls, iron tools, coal, sparks, and smoke.

The blacksmith should appear clearly available to interact with.



MERCHANT
LOCATION:
Adventurer Supply Merchant

SCENE:
The player stands inside a cramped but organized medieval adventuring supply shop.

A merchant stands behind a wooden counter facing the player.

Shelves contain potion bottles, lanterns, rope, bedrolls, backpacks, torches, herbs, empty bottles, food supplies, tools, maps, and miscellaneous dungeon equipment.

The shop feels practical rather than luxurious.

Warm lantern lighting.



HEALER
LOCATION:
Town Healer Sanctuary

SCENE:
The player stands inside a small medieval sanctuary used for healing wounded adventurers.

A healer stands near a simple stone altar.

Behind the healer is a larger sacred resurrection altar surrounded by candles and subtle magical light.

Beds and basic medical supplies sit along the walls.

The location should communicate both ordinary healing and powerful resurrection magic.

Calm, quiet, sacred atmosphere.



TAVERN:
LOCATION:
Adventurer Tavern and Inn

SCENE:
The player stands inside a warm medieval tavern frequented by dungeon explorers.

A bartender stands behind the main counter.

Heavy wooden tables,
a fireplace,
tankards,
travel packs,
weapons resting against chairs,
and several adventurers occupy the room.

A staircase leads toward upstairs rooms.

Warm firelight contrasts strongly with the darkness associated with the dungeon.



BACK ALLEY:
LOCATION:
Hidden Town Back Alley

SCENE:
The player looks down a narrow alley between medieval buildings.

The alley is visible enough that an observant player would investigate it, but it does not appear to be an important town destination at first glance.

Crates,
barrels,
dripping gutters,
old posters,
trash,
and uneven cobblestones line the passage.

A faint lantern glows deeper inside the alley.

Near the far end is a partially hidden doorway or covered gambling table hinting that someone operates back here.


GAMBLER:
LOCATION:
Back Alley Gambler

SCENE:
The player has discovered a shady gambling setup hidden behind the main town buildings.

A charismatic rogue-like gambler sits behind a worn wooden table facing the player.

Dice,
playing cards,
coins,
small locked boxes,
strange relics,
and unidentified equipment cover the table.

A single hanging lantern illuminates the gambler while the surrounding alley remains darker.

The scene should feel secretive and slightly dangerous, but not like a major criminal headquarters.


DUNGEON ENTRANCE:
LOCATION:
Dungeon Entrance

SCENE:
The player stands before the ancient dungeon entrance beneath the town.

A massive stone stairway descends into darkness.

Old reinforced doors,
iron gates,
weathered warning markers,
broken weapons,
discarded packs,
old blood stains,
and extinguished torches show that many adventurers have entered before.

Warm town light remains behind the player viewpoint while colder darkness dominates the descent.

The entrance should immediately communicate:
this is where the real game begins.
