# Authored room dungeon playtest

Double-click **Play Authored Room Dungeon.cmd** in the project folder.

- **Continue** resumes this playtest's own autosave.
- **New random dungeon** creates another arrangement of the room kit.
- **Reviewed crypt floor** uses the same floor layout shown in the review gallery.

The playtest starts in the Ancient Crypts with a prepared three-person party and supplies. Its saves are separate from your main journey.

Use **W/S** to step, **A/D** to turn, **E** to interact, **M** for the map, and **F5** to save. Find the floor key, follow the long vaulted approach, defeat the guardian, and open the ward before taking the stairs. Side routes include supplies, shrines and optional locked chambers. The map reveals the floor as you explore.

The kit contains 25 editable Blender designs. Each new floor assembles 100 exploration sections plus two approach halls, a guardian chamber and a descent chamber. The campaign retains its original enemies, regional materials and themed stairs.

Main campaign saves keep already explored, occupied and completed floor layouts. New journeys and untouched floors use the authored rooms when the updated build is installed. This preserves progress instead of rerolling a floor underneath a saved party.

The gallery distinguishes actual Unreal screenshots from Blender asset previews. Static screenshots cannot establish the absence of monitor tearing or flicker during movement.
