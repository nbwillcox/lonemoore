Combat sound library

Original 8-bit pulse/noise and 16-bit sample-table effects for all seven classes. Tick-stepped sweeps, clocked noise, short arpeggios and compact echoes evoke console JRPG combat. Metal, fire, ice and shadow have separate identities, as do defending, dodging and critical hits. All files use a 16-bit PCM container for game playback.
