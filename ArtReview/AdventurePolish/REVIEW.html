A more focused journey

Named characters and separate saves, tighter dungeon floors, a checkpoint after each boss, better grounded enemies, and distinct combat sounds.

What changed

Your character, your saves

Start as any of the seven classes, name your character, and keep multiple manual saves. Autosaves and quicksaves belong to the selected character.

Less empty travel

New dungeon floors use 68 sections instead of 104. The four-section boss approach, arena, and descent branch remains part of the route.

A checkpoint after the boss

The descent shrine saves your journey and provides a return destination from town after the required boss and key gates are cleared.

More useful treasure

Ordinary chests draw from the item catalog without filtering by the current party. Deeper floors favor better quality, and full bags leave the chest unclaimed.

Stronger dungeon atmosphere

Dimmer player lighting and enemy feet aligned to the floor refine the existing room art and themes.

Recognizable combat feedback

Metallic attacks, a punchy retro fireball, glassy ice, electric strikes, healing chimes, and darker magic replace shared generic spell feedback.
